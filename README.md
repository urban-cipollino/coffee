# Coffee Machine Pet Project
A command line app that simulates a coffee machine. No real state, only in-memory during a session

## Entities
### Drink
1. Espresso 150
2. Americano 180
3. Latte 220

### Ingredient
1. Beans
2. Water
3. Milk
4. Sugar

### Recipe
#### Espresso:
Ingredients:
1. beans 8
2. water 30

Steps:
1. grind
2. tamp
3. brew 25s

#### Americano:
Price: 180

Ingredients:
1. beans 8
2. water 120

Steps:
1. grind
2. brew 25s
3. add water

#### Latte:
Price: 220

Ingredients:
1. beans 8
2. water 30
3. milk 150

Steps:
1. grind
2. brew 25s
3. steam milk
4. mix
